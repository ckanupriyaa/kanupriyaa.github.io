File No. 01 — Data & Growth Platform · 2023–2024

Hyperlocal Geo-Targeting Platform

Vericast · Senior Product Owner

GLBA · FCRA · TCPA Compliant

"Financial institution clients had no visibility into which branches needed which products — marketing was one-size-fits-all and spend was wasted on wrong audiences."

Problem
Bank and credit union clients ran identical campaigns across every branch, with no way to tie digital ad spend to local product opportunity. GA4 data sat unused in BigQuery, account holder and loan data were locked in CRM, and Meta audience data was disconnected from branch geography — leaving marketing ROI unmeasurable at the branch level.
My Role
Owned the end-to-end product and data pipeline — defined branch-level targeting logic, HCMA validation and segmentation rules, and the print-vs-digital media allocation model, while overseeing the engineering build and campaign execution across print, social, and digital channels.
Solution
Designed and shipped a 6-stage data pipeline: (1) ingested GA4 web data via BigQuery, account holder data, loan application data, and Facebook/Meta API audience data into one pipeline, (2) loaded that data into HCMA — the internal campaign management platform — to validate accuracy, completeness, and compliance, and segment audiences by business rule, (3) loaded validated, segmented data from HCMA into a Lakehouse as the single source of truth by branch, (4) connected Tableau to the Lakehouse to build branch-level opportunity-score reports, layering in Narrative BI and Storydoc for client-facing storytelling, (5) built a media allocation model splitting print vs. social vs. digital spend by branch and product opportunity, and (6) executed the campaigns: customized carousel ads on Meta, plus coordinated print and social creative per branch.

File No. 02 — Digital Collections & Compliance · 2023–2025

AI-Enabled Two-Way SMS Mortgage Collections Platform

U.S. Bank · Product Manager

TCPA · FDCPA · CFPB +4 More

"Phone-based right-party contact had declined year over year, and borrowers had limited ways to engage with collections outside the traditional call and IVR channels."

Problem
Mortgage collections relied heavily on outbound phone calls and IVR. Right-party contact by phone had declined roughly 6.15% year over year, borrowers had no asynchronous way to respond, and agents spent time on routine administrative follow-ups instead of complex cases needing real judgment. The bank needed a compliant digital channel — one that handled consent, timing, message status, and jurisdiction-specific rules consistently.
My Role
Defined the product vision and business case for a two-way SMS channel, owned the roadmap and backlog, and translated borrower journeys into epics, features, user stories, and acceptance criteria. Partnered closely with engineering, mortgage servicing, operations, legal, compliance, and security throughout.
Solution
Built a two-way SMS platform on AWS Pinpoint (message delivery), integrated via Apigee with CACS (the collections servicing system), Salesforce (CRM/agent visibility), and the existing IVR channel. Delivered five borrower journeys — promise-to-pay, agent connection, callback requests, payment reminders, and missed-promise follow-up — with message-status tracking (sent, delivered, failed) and opt-in/opt-out management. Defined testable compliance requirements for consent, communication timing, and jurisdiction-specific rules across CA, CT, DC, MA, NH, TX, and NYC.

File No. 03 — Enterprise CRM Modernization · 2020–2023

Salesforce CRM Platform Decomposition

Wells Fargo · Product Owner

4 Lines of Business

"The existing CRM had a monolithic architecture, which made it difficult for different lines of business to customize and release functionality independently."

Problem
Wells Fargo's legacy Salesforce CRM ("CRV") was a monolithic codebase — shared functionality was tightly coupled, so no line of business could customize capabilities, reuse common functionality, or release changes without risking every other business unit's experience. Banker and customer experience grew inconsistent, and the platform couldn't scale.
My Role
Defined the product vision and multi-quarter modernization strategy, owned and evolved the roadmap, and managed the backlog across multiple Scrum teams. Served as the primary liaison among technology teams, senior business stakeholders, and LOB leaders — influencing senior leadership to align on competing platform priorities.
Solution
Led the decomposition of the monolith into 10+ modular, reusable Salesforce capability packages — including Customer 360, Customer Search, Financial Accounts, Click-to-Call, Plan My Day, and Referral Management — serving Consumer Banking, Wealth & Investment Management, Home Lending, and Small Business. Established packaging governance covering installation protocols, versioning standards, pilot/validation processes, production-support hierarchy, and rollout procedures, then led pilot strategy, UAT, change management, and production rollout with each LOB's business owners.

File No. 04 — Loan Origination Automation · 2018–2020

RPA-Enabled Loan Document Verification (UiPath)

KeyBank · Senior Business Analyst

Federal & AML Regulations

"Manual verification and collation of loan documents was time-consuming and error-prone, slowing loan origination and adding risk across screening, validation, and prequalification."

Problem
Loan origination staff manually verified and collated documents across the Loan Origination System (LOS), a process that was slow, inconsistent, and error-prone across submission, verification, and prequalification — all while needing to stay aligned with federal and AML regulations at every step.
My Role
Worked as the bridge between the business and the RPA design team — ran discovery and JAD sessions to capture pain points, performed gap analysis against the proposed automated workflow, and translated business requirements into epics and user stories in JIRA alongside the Product Owner.
Solution
Partnered with RPA design leads to scope a UiPath desktop-automation solution integrated with the LOS via XML/SOAP web services. Documented the future-state process through use case, state, activity, and sequence diagrams and wireframes in Visio, built data mapping and ER diagrams between legacy and new systems, and validated data integrity with SQL queries. The resulting workflow supported underwriting decisions at every stage — origination, screening, validation, and loan management — while reducing manual, error-prone document handling.
Process & Risk Impact
  • Reduced manual effort in loan document verification, collation, and submission
  • Cut human-related errors across the document submission and validation steps
  • Automated end-to-end underwriting support from origination through loan management
  • Kept federal and AML regulatory requirements embedded across every workflow step
Delivery & Adoption
